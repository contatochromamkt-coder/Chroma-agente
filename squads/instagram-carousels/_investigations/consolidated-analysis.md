## Universal Patterns

### Structural Patterns
- **Myth-busting / contrarian-reveal structure** appears across 3 of 5 profiles (anagcouto, pedrosobral, priscila_zillo): name a common belief → show it's incomplete or wrong → reveal the "real" answer. This is the single strongest cross-profile carousel structure signal.
- **Numbered-listicle structure** ("N coisas que...", "N frases que...") appears prominently in pedrosobral and is echoed in leandroladeiran's numbered-step framework Reel. High engagement, easy to swipe, easy to produce.
- **Reels dominate current posting mix** for 3 of 5 profiles (leandroladeiran, tay.ldantas, and to a lesser extent priscila_zillo/anagcouto) even on accounts known for carousels — carousels are increasingly a secondary format relative to Reels across this niche, but still drive strong engagement when used with the right structure.
- **Closing "follow me" or CTA slide** as the final carousel slide is an explicit pattern in pedrosobral; anagcouto and priscila_zillo instead favor a softer close (reflective line or implicit "keep swiping") on thought-leadership content, while comment-to-unlock CTAs dominate lead-magnet content (anagcouto).

### Language Patterns
- **Second-person, conversational Portuguese** ("você") is shared across all 5 profiles, mixing business vocabulary with everyday/slang phrasing (e.g., "duro", "cara triste", "deu ruim" in pedrosobral) — none of the profiles use a stiff, corporate tone even when discussing serious business topics.
- **Naming a concept/framework explicitly** (Tay Dantas's "efeito Diderot", pedrosobral's numbered "coisas") gives content a shareable, memorable hook — found in 3 of 5 profiles.
- **Short captions, depth lives in the slides/video** — found in tay.ldantas, priscila_zillo, and pedrosobral: captions rarely exceed 2-3 sentences; the actual teaching happens in the carousel slides or reel video, not the caption text.

### Engagement Drivers
- **Comment-to-unlock CTAs** ("comente 'palavra' e receba...") reliably spike comment volume — confirmed in anagcouto (23-55 comments vs 13 without it) and echoed by leandroladeiran's "Comenta EU QUERO" pattern. This is the strongest, most repeatable engagement lever found across profiles.
- **Personal/emotional storytelling outperforms pure business content** on at least 2 profiles (priscila_zillo's Father's Day post vastly outperformed her business carousel; leandroladeiran's meme/relatable Reel had the best comment-to-like ratio) — an emotional or relatable on-ramp before the teaching content consistently boosts engagement.
- **Current-events/news-jacking content drives more comments (debate) but not necessarily more likes** (pedrosobral's news-jacking carousel: lowest likes, highest comments of his sample) — useful for reach/algorithmic signals even when it doesn't top the like count.

## Recommended Framework

### Structure Template
1. **Opening/Hook**: Use one of three validated hook templates, chosen per content angle:
   - **Myth-bust**: "[Crença comum] já virou [conselho/regra quase proibida]... mas isso está incompleto." (pedrosobral, anagcouto, priscila_zillo)
   - **Relatable question**: "Você já teve isso? [experiência comum]..." before naming the concept being taught (tay.ldantas)
   - **Numbered-contrast**: "N coisas que fazem sentido agora que [resultado desejado] (mas antes não faziam)" (pedrosobral)
2. **Body Structure**: 6-10 slides. Slide 2-3 = concrete example/context (use real numbers/prices when possible, per leandroladeiran). Slides 4-8 = one teaching point per slide (numbered or sequential argument-build). Avoid the 2-slide format (anagcouto's shortest sample) — evidence suggests 6-10 slides deliver more complete value.
3. **Supporting Elements**: Anchor abstract claims with concrete numbers, named frameworks/concepts (give the idea a memorable "label"), and real examples — never generic tips without specificity.
4. **Closing/CTA**: Two validated CTA templates:
   - **Comment-to-unlock** (for lead-magnet/gated content): "Comenta '[PALAVRA-CHAVE]' e recebe [oferta]" — strongest comment-driver found.
   - **Follow + save** (for evergreen educational content): closing slide with a short value promise + "Me segue para [benefício]" plus caption line "Já salva pra não esquecer."

### Voice Guidelines
- **Tone**: Educational per Chroma Marketing's brief, grounded in the second-person, conversational-but-authoritative "mentor" voice shared across all 5 profiles. Avoid corporate/stiff phrasing.
- **Vocabulary to adopt**: "você", concrete numbers/prices, "estratégia", "resultado", named-concept labels (e.g., "efeito X"), short punchy sentence starters.
- **Vocabulary to avoid**: vague superlatives with no payoff ("a melhor aula da sua vida" without substance — leandroladeiran's weakest post), generic corporate jargon, over-long unbroken paragraphs.
- **Sentence style**: Short, scannable lines with frequent line breaks; one idea per slide.
- **Emoji and formatting**: Light, precise emoji use (not emoji-heavy) is consistent with higher-follower profiles in this sample (tay.ldantas, leandroladeiran use emoji sparingly in captions).

### Hook Templates
1. **Myth-bust reveal**: "'[Conselho comum]' já virou regra. Mas isso está incompleto." — Source: @anagcouto/@pedrosobral/@priscila_zillo. Why it works: reframes accepted wisdom, creates curiosity gap.
2. **Relatable question + named concept**: "Você já teve isso? [experiência]... Isso tem nome: [conceito]." — Source: @tay.ldantas, 513 comments. Why it works: personal identification before teaching.
3. **Numbered contrast**: "N coisas que fazem sentido agora que [resultado] (mas antes não faziam)" — Source: @pedrosobral, 2.8k likes. Why it works: before/after framing plus concrete list promise.
4. **Practical searchable question**: "Como [resolver problema prático específico]?" — Source: @priscila_zillo, 7k likes/135 comments. Why it works: direct, high-intent question mirrors search behavior.
5. **News-jacking hook**: "[Fato surpreendente/atual]. Vem entender o que isso ensina sobre [tema]." — Source: @pedrosobral, 318 comments (highest comment count in sample). Why it works: current events drive debate/reach.

### CTA Templates
1. **Comment-to-unlock**: "Comenta '[PALAVRA]' e recebe [oferta/material]" — Avg engagement lift: comment counts roughly 2x higher when present (anagcouto, leandroladeiran evidence).
2. **Follow + value promise**: "Toda semana eu compartilho [tema]. Me segue para [benefício]." — closing-slide template, proven recurring pattern (pedrosobral).
3. **Save + reflect**: "Já salva pra não esquecer." / question CTA ("Pelo que você trabalha pra ter? Comenta aqui.") — best for practical listicle content (pedrosobral).

### Anti-Patterns
1. **Vague superlative hooks with no follow-through**: leandroladeiran's weakest post ("a melhor aula de vendas da sua vida") underperformed both other posts on every metric — hooks must be backed by specific content.
2. **Overly short carousels (2 slides)**: anagcouto's 2-slide carousel is thin relative to the 6-10 slide norm found elsewhere — under-delivers on educational depth.
3. **Forcing a hard CTA onto thought-leadership content**: mismatching CTA style to content intent (e.g., comment-to-unlock on a purely reflective post) reads as inauthentic — match CTA to content purpose.
4. **Ignoring personal/emotional on-ramps entirely**: profiles that only post pure business/tactical content (without any relatable or emotional framing) show comparatively lower engagement than posts blending personal storytelling with the lesson.
